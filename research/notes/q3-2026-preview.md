## Why ads are decelerating → thesis impact
- **Mostly macro** (demand-side): China consumption soft, ecommerce ad budgets specifically cut. Plus a harder comp after Q2's +28%.
- **Thesis impact:** pressures the near-term FY26 bridge and can **delay the re-rate**, but doesn't break the structural case. Break line (ad <15% for 2 quarters) still clear at +20%. Watch the *trajectory*.

## Where the OTHER-WAY edge is (ad upside offsets — BILI-specific, macro-independent)
- **Ad load ~7% → low-teens** runway (structural inventory, doesn't need macro). Biggest lever.
- **New scenarios:** search (doubled YoY), OTT/big-screen, PC, mini-program, Story/vertical — +50% YoY multi-scenario in H1.
- **AI eCPM:** full-funnel matching +3% in Q1 trials, +5% H2 target — pure efficiency.
- **Secular verticals offsetting cyclical:** AI advertisers +100%+, auto/home/apparel +60%+ — directly offsetting the ecommerce cut.
- **Breadth:** advertiser count +14%; automated tools onboarding long-tail/SME.
- **Not in numbers:** iOS China App Store commission cut (JPM ~3% positive).
- **Leading indicators to monitor:** monthly active advertiser count, search/OTT monetisation ramp, ad-load trajectory, eCPM disclosures, AI-vertical growth rate, Double-11 read-through.
