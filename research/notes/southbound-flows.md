# Southbound Flows & Positioning — What to Track

The leg-2 re-rate catalyst is a *flow* story. Here's the framework and the data to actually pull.

## The positioning spectrum (underweight → neutral → overweight)
This is about **global active funds' allocation to China vs their benchmark weight**:
- **Underweight** = hold *less* China than the benchmark. (What active funds did 2021–2025 — a 4-year run.)
- **Benchmark-neutral** = hold *exactly* benchmark weight. (Where BofA's Sept 2026 FMS says funds moved in **June 2026**, ending the underweight.)
- **Overweight** = hold *more* than benchmark — active *buying*. (Not there yet — the bullish leg still ahead.)

**Why it matters — the sequence:**
1. **Sellers stop** (underweight → neutral): removes the supply that suppressed multiples for 4 years. *Happening now.* "China needs the sellers to stop, not global investors to turn bullish."
2. **Passive catch-up** (mechanical): as the benchmark re-weights, passive follows.
3. **Active overweight** (neutral → overweight): the big money returning — needs a catalyst (earnings proof or policy). **This is the missing leg** both Goldman and the Substack flag as still absent.

## How to read it for BILI specifically
- BILI is **southbound-eligible** → the southbound bid lands on **9626.HK** (not the ADR — relevant if this becomes a long-term hold; see [positioning.md](positioning.md)).
- The three-legged flow case: **southbound (persistent domestic)** + **active underweight unwinding (one-time supply removal)** + **corporate buybacks (BILI's own + the RMB 4.1tn sector cash-return theme)**.
- **The turn signal to wait for:** BofA FMS showing funds going *overweight* (from neutral), EPFR *active* inflows positive, HF net exposure rising. That's "Western active money returning" — the leg that converts "sellers stopped" into an actual re-rate.
- **Caveat:** southbound alone is price-insensitive and can distort; it's a bid, not a valuation anchor. The durable re-rate needs the active-money leg, which is un-timeable.
