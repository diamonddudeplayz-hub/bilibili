# Tencent Deal — The Overhang Resolution (Sept–Nov 2026)

**Bottom line:** the Tencent overhang (leg-2 technical catalyst) is **substantially resolved** — and via a structure *better* than the original "wait for a Q4 block trade" base case. Float was shrunk, not just transferred. **Completed 5 Nov 2026.**

## What happened (timeline)
- **Sep 4, 2026** — Priced: **US$700M zero-coupon convertible notes due 2031** ($500M marketed + $200M taken by Tencent). Conversion price **HK$155.79** (28.3% premium to the Sep 4 close of HK$121.40; 35% above the placement reference). No coupon, no accretion.
- **Concurrent equity placement** at reference **HK$115.38**: Tencent secondary of 26,374,900 shares + 6,976,760 borrowed shares for convert-arb delta.
- **Concurrent buyback (special US$300M program), shares cancelled (Rule 8A.15):** US$100M delta repurchase (6,795,540 sh) + **US$200M straight from Tencent (13,591,090 sh)**.
- **Oct 28, 2026** — EGM poll passed (¾ of disinterested shareholders).
- **Nov 5, 2026** — Tencent Notes Subscription + Concurrent Tencent Repurchase **closed**.

## Why it beats a plain block trade
1. **Overhang resolved AND float shrunk in one move.** A normal block just hands Tencent's stock to a new buyer — overhang transfers, doesn't disappear. Here **~US$300M was retired permanently** (EPS-accretive); the rest went to fresh long-only placement buyers.
2. **Tencent stayed partially aligned.** They rolled $200M into the 2031 convert at a 28% higher strike (HK$155.79) instead of fully exiting — only become equity holders again ~50% above here.
3. **Self-engineered catalyst.** Zero-coupon converts (no cash interest) funding buybacks that cancel Tencent stock. The overhang is no longer an exogenous event to *wait* for.

## The honest cost / caveats
- **Convert-arb short pressure.** The delta/placement deliberately created borrowed-share shorts so arb investors hedge the notes — ongoing mechanical short flow post-deal. (But the data shows BILI still *outperformed* HSTECH since Sep 4, so this is a minor residual vs sector beta — see [positioning.md](positioning.md).)
- **Buyback paused into earnings.** Two overlapping pauses: the CB lock-up (cleared on completion, 5 Nov) and the pre-earnings blackout (until ~1–2 trading days after the 24 Nov print). **Buyback bid should resume ~late Nov** — a near-term technical tailwind.
- **NOT FCF-funded.** H1'26 FCF was only RMB 995M (AI capex front-load) — can't cover $300M+ buyback + capex. It's **convert-funded** (leverage, clever zero-coupon, but not spare cash). See the cash-flow notes in [q3-2026-preview.md](q3-2026-preview.md).

## Effect on share count / EPS
