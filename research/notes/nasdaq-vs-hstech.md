## What this means
1. **China tech has decoupled from US tech.** They moved together in 2019–21 (0.59) when global liquidity lifted everything; since the 2021 crackdown + geopolitics + "ex-China" mandate exodus, HSTECH detached and now trades on its **own domestic/policy factor** (recent corr ~0). The decoupling *is* the "ex-China" regime visible in the data.
2. **BILI is a genuine diversifier, not "more tech beta."** For a book already long US AI/tech (i.e. everyone), adding China tech is **decorrelated** exposure — the drivers (China policy, southbound flows, the China risk premium) are orthogonal to US rates + megacap-AI earnings. This earns BILI a place at *modest* conviction, independent of the stock-specific call.
3. **The asymmetry.** One index is priced for perfection at highs; the other is dead money at a cheap base. Classic "replace beta with alpha / buy the de-rated thing." **Honest counterweight:** "cheap + uncorrelated" has been a value trap for 4–5 years running — low correlation + flat returns means it hasn't *paid* to own it. The bet is a regime change (see [positioning.md](positioning.md) §4).
4. **Reinforces single-name over index.** HSTECH itself compounded terribly (+27% in 12 yrs) — it's the mega-platforms + the food-delivery war. If you want the China re-rate *without* the index's structural earnings drag, you want a name whose **earnings actually inflect** (BILI) so you're not relying solely on a multiple that's gone nowhere for a decade.
