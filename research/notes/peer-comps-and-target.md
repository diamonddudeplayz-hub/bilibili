# Peer Comps, NetEase vs BILI, and the Q3 → Target Linkage

Sources: "Chinese Tech Is Cheap. How Much of the Cash Reaches You?" (Primary Source Arbitrage, 30 Sep 2026); our Q3 build. Scorecard: `research/q3_2026_scorecard.xlsx`.

## The jurisdiction discount (why "cheap")
- HK internet trades at a **44–46% discount** to US peers **at matched growth and margins**. Growth does not explain the gap.
- Not weak payouts: HK names return **more** cash than US (JD 3.8% div, NetEase ~2.9% total yield, Tencent 2.4% vs Meta 1.4%, Alphabet 1.9%). The "China hoards cash" narrative has been false since ~2023.
- The discount prices **three risks**: VIE (low–med), cash repatriation (low, demonstrably de-risked since 2022), **geopolitical tail (med–high — the one the market weights most).** The market is pricing all three "as though 2022 conditions still hold."
- Flow confirmation (2nd source, alongside Goldman): **BofA Sept 2026 FMS** — active long-only funds moved to **benchmark-neutral on China in June, ending a 4-year underweight.** The marginal *seller* is out → supply overhang that suppressed multiples for 4 years is lifting. "China needs the sellers to stop, not global investors to turn bullish."

## NetEase vs BILI — NOT interchangeable (answers "can we buy any name?")
| | NetEase | Bilibili |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | 18.9% | 18.3% (matches Meta) |
| EBIT margin | **40%** (≈ Meta 41%) | **5%** |
| Fwd P/E | 11.7x | 13.9x |
| Discount type | **Pure jurisdiction** (46% vs Meta, both US-listed) | Jurisdiction **+ margin-expansion bet** |
| Bet count | **One** (discount compresses) | **Two** (discount compresses AND margins inflect) |
| While you wait | ~2.9% shareholder yield | no yield |
| Mean target implies | ~17x (halfway compression, +43%) | ~24x = needs margin **and** jurisdiction |

**Takeaway:** NetEase is the clean, lower-variance expression of *just* the China re-rate (matched fundamentals to Meta, margins already in hand). BILI already prices margin expansion into its 13.9x — you're paying up on 5% current margins, so you need your margin call to deliver *on top of* the re-rate. **Different risk shapes, same tailwind.** Sophisticated construction = **core-satellite within China**: NetEase as the jurisdiction-compression anchor (pays you to wait), BILI as the margin-inflection satellite (the alpha). If you don't back your BILI margin call, NetEase is the more honest vehicle.
