## The honest risks (what you're really betting on)
This is **two bets stacked**:
1. **BILI's earnings inflect** — HIGH conviction (your edge; the model is well-calibrated per Q2 est-vs-actual).
2. **The China regime turns so the market *pays* for those earnings** — LOW conviction, un-timeable (Goldman's call). The 12-year data is brutal: HSTECH +27% total, decorrelated from the US tech bull. "What the company does" hasn't mattered for 4–5 years — the sector trades on policy/flows.

Plus: your FY27 EPS (11.12) is ~28–44% above the bulls — the HK$232 target is doubly levered to gaming *and* margin *and* a re-rate. And the Q3 ad print is a coin-flip into a hostile tape.
