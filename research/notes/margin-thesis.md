# The Margin Work — Why Our Numbers Sit Above the Street

This is the core of owning BILI over NetEase: BILI is the **margin-expansion bet**. Our FY27 adj EPS (RMB 11.12) is ~28–44% above the bulls, and the gap is almost entirely **margin flow-through** (adj net margin 13.4% vs street ~10%), plus a lower share count. So the whole thesis rests on: *why do our margins make sense?*

Margin trajectory in our model: GM 36.6% (FY25) → 37.8% (FY26) → **39.9% (FY27)**; adj net margin 8.5% → 9.6% → **13.4%**.

## Why the margins make sense (3 structural reasons)
1. **Gross margin keeps climbing because content cost is structurally FIXED.** Bilibili's PUGV content is **creator-subsidised** — creators produce at their own cost, so Bilibili's content COGS does *not* scale with revenue. Meanwhile **advertising** (the fastest-growing, now-largest stream, 39% of revenue vs 23% in FY21) carries **near-100% incremental gross margin** (the content exists whether or not an ad is served). So every point of mix shift toward ads mechanically lifts blended GM. This isn't a forecast — it's a **16-consecutive-quarter observed trend** (17.6% FY22 → 37.2% Q2'26). The street models this curve *flattening* (~38% terminal); we carry it toward management's stated **40–45%** target because the ad-mix shift still has room to run.
2. **Operating leverage: opex grows slower than revenue because the community self-sustains.** S&M is falling as a % of revenue — organic/community growth means less paid user acquisition. **Q2 proof:** revenue +8%, but S&M **+1%** (and it came in *below* our own estimate, 13.4% vs 14.7% modelled), G&A flat; gross profit +10% > opex +7% = +100bps adj OPM. We model opex growing ~6% on mid-teens revenue; the street models it closer to revenue growth.
3. **Management target is stated and the trajectory is tracking it.** Target: 40–45% GPM, 15–20% OPM. Current 37.2% / 8.8%. We reach the low end by FY27; the street stops short.

## Why the street is conservative (and our counter)
- **Street haircuts for:** (i) AI capex / R&D drag (R&D +16%, RMB 1B AI capex); (ii) macro ad weakness slowing the high-margin stream; (iii) conservatism on whether GM plateaus.
- **Our counter:** (i) AI capex is mostly *capitalised* (PPE), and the P&L R&D drag is **bounded** (~RMB 500M, flagged, 70–80% deployed H1) — a known headwind that operating leverage overwhelmed in Q2; (ii) even with ad decelerating to ~20%, ad still grows faster than the blended top line → mix shift continues; (iii) 16 quarters of evidence + the fixed-cost structure.

## The honest dependency (what to monitor)
The FY27 margin **step-up is intertwined with gaming**: the big leverage comes from the top line *reaccelerating to ~+15%* (gaming inflection) against a controlled cost base. So the margin bet is **not fully independent** of the gaming bet — the FY27 operating leverage needs the gaming revenue to actually show up. And the R&D/AI line is the live headwind (Q2 R&D ran hot, 12.7% vs our 12.0%).

**Monitor each quarter:** (1) is **gross profit growing faster than opex** (leverage intact)? (2) is **S&M staying disciplined** (% of revenue flat/down)? (3) is **gaming revenue showing up** to give the top line to leverage? If GM plateaus at ~38% or opex tracks revenue, our 13.4% net margin compresses toward the street's 10% and the target comes down materially — this is the single biggest swing factor in the EPS, so it's the single most important thing to watch.
